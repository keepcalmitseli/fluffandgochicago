Meet Your Pet Stylist

Welcome to Fluff & Go Chicago. I'm Vernell, a certified professional pet stylist dedicated to providing dogs with quality grooming and personalized care.


Grooming isn't just about making a dog look good. It's also about creating a comfortable experience, maintaining proper hygiene, and treating every pet with patience and respect.


My goal is simple: give every dog the attention they deserve while giving their owners a grooming experience they can feel confident about.
